Keeping Your Flint HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of the year.

Minor issues don't stay minor when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
